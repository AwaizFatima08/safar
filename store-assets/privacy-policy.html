Urdu Safar — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

What this app does

Urdu Safar is a study app for O Level Urdu (Second Language, Cambridge syllabus 3248) exam practice: vocabulary drills, reading and grammar exercises, translation practice, and writing prompts.

Data collection

Urdu Safar does not collect any personal information. The app does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email, or any identifying information, and does not connect to any server while you use it.

Data stored on your device

Your practice progress — scores, stars, flashcard review schedule, and completed exercises — is saved only in the app's local storage on your own device. This data:

  • never leaves your device;
  • is not transmitted to us or to any third party;
  • is deleted automatically if you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Third-party services

Urdu Safar does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services of any kind.

Children's privacy

Urdu Safar is suitable for students of any age preparing for O Level exams. Since the app collects no personal data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

If Urdu Safar's data practices change in a future update (for example, if an optional account feature is added to sync progress across devices), this page will be updated first, and the change will be described here before it takes effect.
