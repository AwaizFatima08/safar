Content and accuracy

Urdu Safar's practice content covers vocabulary, reading, writing, and grammar topics common to both the Cambridge O Level Urdu (Second Language, 3248) and IGCSE Urdu (Foreign Language, 0539) syllabi, as understood at the time of writing, for exam-practice purposes. The two syllabi differ in some exam components (for example, exact paper structure), so it is a study aid, not an official Cambridge publication, and is provided without warranty of exam outcome — always check the current official syllabus and past papers for your specific qualification from Cambridge International as your primary reference.
