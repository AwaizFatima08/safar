Urdu Safar — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

No account is required to use this app. An account is only created if you choose to sign up.

What this app does

Urdu Safar is a study app for Cambridge O Level and IGCSE Urdu exam practice: vocabulary drills, reading and grammar exercises, translation practice, and writing prompts. It also offers an optional friend-group leaderboard.

Using the app as a guest (no account)

By default, Urdu Safar works entirely offline. You pick a nickname (stored only on your device) to track your own progress and appear on a leaderboard saved only on that device. No email, password, or other personal information is collected, and nothing is sent to any server.

Data collected if you create an account

Creating an account is entirely optional, and only needed if you want to create or join a friend group whose leaderboard syncs across devices. If you sign up, we collect and store, using Firebase (a Google Cloud service):

  • Email address and password — used only to sign you in. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store your password ourselves.
  • Display name — the nickname you chose, shown to other members of your friend group.
  • Practice scores (gold/silver/bronze medal counts) — shown to other members of any friend group you join.
  • Friend group membership — which group(s) you've joined, and the group's invite code.

This data is visible only to members of the same friend group — never made public, never shared with advertisers, and never used for anything other than making the friend-group leaderboard work.

Data stored on your device

Regardless of whether you have an account, your detailed practice progress — which exercises you've completed, flashcard review schedule, and so on — is saved in the app's local storage on your own device and is not uploaded anywhere. Only the summary medal counts described above are synced, and only if you're signed in and in a friend group.

Data deletion

If you have an account, you can permanently delete it and all data associated with it (email, display name, synced scores, group membership) at any time from the app's Account screen. If you no longer have the app installed, you can request deletion by emailing homi55@gmail.com from your account's registered email address — see delete-account.html for details. Your on-device guest progress is separate and is removed whenever you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Third-party services

Urdu Safar uses Firebase (Google Cloud) solely to provide sign-in and the friend-group leaderboard described above. The app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services of any kind.

Children's privacy

Urdu Safar is aimed at O Level students, typically aged 14 and older, and is not directed at young children. Friend groups are invite-code-only (never publicly searchable), and the app has no chat or messaging feature.

Changes to this policy

If Urdu Safar's data practices change further, this page will be updated first, and the change will be described here before it takes effect.
