# Urdu Safar v1.2 — scope (locked 2026-11-07)

User request: "add further topics to enhance syllabus coverage in the same style".
Approved in chat: all 20 topics below, then a v1.2 Play build (versionCode 7,
versionName 1.2).

## Why these 20

The 37 topics after v1.1 lean heavily on places, heritage, nature and safety.
Themes with no topic yet: work and careers, money and shopping, food and daily
routine, online life, social workers and scientists, and current public issues
(floods, smog, waste). The writing tasks had no complaint letter, speech, diary,
story or interview write-up. The Reading Skills texts had no menu, timetable, job
advert, website page or form.

## Content: 20 new topics (37 → 57)

Same full treatment as v1.1: Essay topic (10 vocab words, reading MCQs, writing
drills, grammar point), Reading Skills (short answer + multiple matching +
note-making), Grammar Lab (cloze + 5 exam-format stem transformations),
Translation (warm-up + passage), plus a standalone Summary Writing passage and
an Extended Writing task with 3 points. All text is original.

## Facts

Facts are kept to well-established ones (dates, places, awards). Where a number
could be disputed it is rounded or left out. People: Edhi (1928–2016, Edhi
Foundation, Karachi), Ruth Pfau (arrived Karachi 1960, Marie Adelaide Leprosy
Centre, leprosy declared controlled 1996, died 2017), Abdus Salam (Nobel Prize
in Physics 1979), Iqbal (born Sialkot 1877, died 1938, Allahabad address 1930).
Space: iCube-Qamar went to the Moon on China's Chang'e-6 mission in May 2024.
