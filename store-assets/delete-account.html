Delete Your Urdu Safar Account

Applies only to a signed-up account (email + password). Guest use of the app needs no account and nothing here applies — just uninstall the app to remove your on-device progress.

Option 1 — In the app (instant)

Open Urdu Safar → Leaderboard → Account → Delete my account. You'll be asked to re-enter your password to confirm. This immediately and permanently deletes your account, your friend-group membership, and every score synced to it.

Option 2 — By email (if you no longer have the app)

Send an email to homi55@gmail.com from the email address your account is registered under, with the subject "Delete my Urdu Safar account." We'll delete your account and all associated data within 30 days and confirm by reply.

Email to request deletion

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email, password)
  • Your display name and synced practice scores
  • Your membership in any friend group

On-device guest progress (if any) is separate and is not affected by account deletion — remove it by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage on your device.
